# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users range from beginners to intermediate/experienced individuals who want to improve their flexibility, mobility, strength, or contortion skills. They might be working toward specific goals like getting their splits, improving back flexibility, or building strength for advanced skills. The experience must make beginners feel welcome without being intimidating, while remaining serious and robust enough for experienced practitioners.

## Product Purpose

JazContortion is a personal movement and flexibility brand that provides structured paid programs alongside free YouTube training. It exists to teach users that flexibility isn't just about passively stretching farther, but about developing the strength, control, consistency, and active skills to actually use their range of motion.

## Positioning

Unlike generic, impersonal fitness or stretching apps, JazContortion is a personal journey. Users learn alongside Jaz, combining contortion, active flexibility, and movement skills in an environment that feels like genuine personal training and content from a real person. 

## Operating Context

Users engage with the platform on the web to access structured training, programs, and free resources. The environment is highly interactive, leveraging scroll-driven reveals, hover states, animated transitions, and layered elements to reflect the movement and physicality of the training itself.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Preservation:** Must refine the existing design rather than redesigning the brand from scratch.
- **Interactivity:** Needs high interactivity (animations, click events, smooth scrolls, directional movement) that feels intentional and connected to the contortion identity, not just decorative.
- **Avoid:** Generic fitness-app aesthetics,  
- **Layout Constraints:** Prioritize personality, curved backgrounds, unique shapes

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Personal, energetic, encouraging, slightly playful, and confident. It must feel like Jaz, not a large fitness company.
- **Visual Identity:** Warm cream/off-white background, near-black/very dark foreground, with Orange as the primary accent. Soft peach/light-orange tones can be used sparingly.
- **Typography:** Large, bold, expressive typography.
- **Aesthetics:** Editorial and asymmetrical layouts with organic shapes, curves, layered/overlapping imagery, and strong visual hierarchy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Next.js web platform with incumbent visual implementation (colors, layout, programs).
- Current brand palette: cream/off-white, black, orange.

## Product Principles

1. **Personal over Corporate:** The experience should feel like following a real person's movement journey, never an impersonal software platform.
2. **Active over Passive:** Training focuses on strength, control, and usable range of motion rather than just passive stretching.
3. **Movement in Design:** The interface must convey physicality and movement through editorial layouts, purposeful asymmetry, and intentional micro-interactions.
4. **Accessible Depth:** The brand must be welcoming to beginners while maintaining the serious rigor expected by experienced practitioners.
